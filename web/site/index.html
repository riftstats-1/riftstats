RiftStats

Your League stats.
Without the weight.

Rank, recent form, LP history and match history in a desktop app of a few megabytes. No ads, no overlays you did not ask for.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 and 11

Recent form at a glance

Your last 20 games as one bar strip, with win rate, KDA and the champions you play most.

LP history that follows you

Riot only shows your LP right now. RiftStats saves every change, so you can see where you are heading.

Light on your PC

Built with Tauri and Rust instead of a bundled browser, so it starts fast and leaves room for the game.
